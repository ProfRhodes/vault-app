# Forgetting curve

Hermann Ebbinghaus measured in the 1880s how quickly memorized material fades without review. Retention drops steeply at first and then levels off. Each review flattens the curve.

**In the app:** notes you have not opened in a while dim, and opening one brightens it again. See [[spreading-activation]] for the other half of recall.

Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forgetting_curve)
